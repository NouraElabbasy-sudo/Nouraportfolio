a media-buying tip carousel that proves expertise through value, with the
creative self-intro folded into slide 1's hook. Designed drafts are published
as a Claude Design canvas — open it, swap in your real headshot/branding if
you want one, then export each slide as PNG and post in order.

## Design system
- Palette: deep navy (#1B2A4A) + warm terracotta accent (#D97757) + cream (#FBF7F0)
- Type: Cairo (headlines, weights 700-900) + Tajawal (body, weights 400-900) — both Arabic-native Google Fonts
- Layout: fully RTL — text right-aligned, icon+text rows flow right-to-left, consistent right-side accent bar and footer across all 4 slides
- Visual system: each "mistake" slide uses a numbered badge (Arabic-Indic numerals ١٢٣), a terracotta ✕ mark next to the problem, and a navy "fix box" with a checkmark for the solution — scannable even muted/no sound
