## Design system
- Palette: deep navy (#1B2A4A) + warm terracotta accent (#D97757) + cream (#FBF7F0), with alternating slide backgrounds (cream → navy → cream → terracotta) so the carousel pops as you swipe instead of reading as one flat block
- Type: Cairo (headlines, 700-900) + Tajawal (body, 400-900) + Lalezar (sticker/accent display) — all Arabic-native Google Fonts
- Layout: fully RTL — text right-aligned, icon+text rows flow right-to-left
- Gen Z visual language: rotated sticker badges with hard offset shadows (comic/meme "pop" look), halftone dot texture, custom flat-shape illustrations tied to each mistake (burning cash, missed dartboard, blasting megaphone, crossed-out message), curiosity/question-form headlines instead of flat statements, casual mixed-tone Arabic copy
- Each "mistake" slide keeps a numbered badge, a short explainer, and a "الصح إيه؟" (what's right?) fix box with a checkmark — scannable even muted/no sound
