# First Post — Launch Carousel (v2: tip carousel, Arabic)

**Format:** Carousel, 4 slides, 1080x1350 (4:5 portrait). Topic: "3 mistakes
that burn your ad budget" (media buying), with the creative self-intro folded
into slide 1's hook instead of a separate intro slide. Slide-by-slide design
brief is in `visual-brief.md`. Design canvas link: see the session artifact
shared with this post (search your Claude Code artifacts for "Noura Launch
Carousel").

Why this beats a plain intro post: tip/value content earns saves and shares,
which is what the algorithm rewards on a first post — and it proves expertise
instead of just claiming it. Your identity (media buyer + corporate comms
lead) is folded into the hook on slide 1, not spent on its own slide.
