# @noura.talks.marketing — Instagram Strategy

## Account snapshot (as of 2026-09-08)
Instagram's public pages aren't reachable from this environment (egress blocked) and
the handle doesn't surface in web search — both consistent with a brand-new account
with no posts yet. This plan is built from your stated positioning rather than a
content audit, since there's no existing content to audit. Once the account has a
few posts, re-run an audit to refine this.

## Who you are (the differentiator)
Most "marketing" accounts only talk theory. You have two things most don't:
1. **Practitioner credibility** — content creation, digital marketing, media buying
   (you run paid budgets, not just post tips).
2. **Corporate-scale experience** — Event & Corporate Communication lead inside a
   multinational company. That's boardroom-level, brand-safe, large-audience
   communication experience most solo creators never get.

The account should position you as: *"A marketer who's actually run the campaigns
and the corporate comms — not just talking about it."* That combination is the
trust wedge that turns viewers into clients/freelance leads.

## Goal
Increase viewers who **trust** the content → convert them into **clients and
freelance project inquiries**. Every content pillar below maps to a stage of that
funnel: Reach → Trust → Conversion.

## Bio (rewrite)
Instagram bios: 150 characters, 4-5 lines read best.

```
Noura | Digital Marketing & Media Buyer
📊 Corporate Comms & Events @ [Company] — real campaigns, real results
🎯 Helping brands grow with strategy that actually converts
👇 Work with me / free resources
```

Notes:
- Line 1: name + role (searchable, clear).
- Line 2: the differentiator (corporate + agency-level experience in one line).
  Keep the company name only if you're allowed to reference it publicly —
  otherwise swap for "multinational corporate comms experience."
- Line 3: outcome-focused value prop, not a job title list.
- Line 4: CTA — link in bio (Linktree/portfolio) pointing to booking form,
  portfolio, or WhatsApp/DM.
- Category tag: set to "Digital creator" + add "Marketing Consultant" as your
  Professional Dashboard category for discoverability.
- Profile photo: clear face photo, good lighting, direct eye contact — trust
  signal #1 before anyone reads a word.

## Content pillars

| # | Pillar | Purpose | Format | Frequency |
|---|--------|---------|--------|-----------|
| 1 | Marketing & media buying tips | Authority / reach | Reels, carousels | 2x/week |
| 2 | Corporate comms & event BTS | Differentiation / trust | Reels, Stories | 1x/week |
| 3 | Case studies & results | Trust / proof | Carousels, single post | 1x/week |
| 4 | Personal brand & career journey | Relatability / trust | Reels, Stories | 1-2x/week |
| 5 | Client work & testimonials | Conversion | Single post, Stories highlight | As earned |

**Why this mix works:** pillars 1-2 build reach and a distinct niche (nobody else
is posting "media buyer who also runs corporate events"). Pillars 3-5 convert that
attention into leads by proving results and lowering risk for a prospective client.

## Posting cadence (sustainable starting point)
- Feed: 3-4 posts/week (mix of Reels and carousels — Reels for reach, carousels for saves/trust)
- Stories: daily, even if just 1-2 (polls, BTS, reposts, CTA stickers)
- Comment/engage: 15-20 min/day on target accounts (brands, event industry, marketing peers) — this drives discovery more than posting volume does

## Trust → conversion mechanics
- Every 3rd-4th post should have a **soft CTA** ("Save this," "Send to a friend who needs this")
- Every 1-2 weeks, a **direct CTA** post ("DM 'GROW' for a free audit," "Booking 2 freelance slots this month")
- Pin your best 3 posts (intro post + 1 case study + 1 results/proof post) so new visitors see credibility immediately
- Build a Highlights row: **Services | Results | About Me | Corporate Work | FAQ**
- Add a Linktree/portfolio link with: portfolio, booking calendar, WhatsApp/DM, testimonials page
